## Platform Selection & 3-Line Justification

**Chosen Platform:** WordPress with WooCommerce

### 3-Line Justification:
1. **Cost:** WooCommerce is an open-source, zero-license-cost platform ideal for small B2C fashion retailers, providing enterprise-level e-commerce features without recurring fees.
2. **Customization:** It offers complete flexibility to customize store design, product attributes (size variants), local shipping methods (EMS), and Bangladeshi mobile payment gateways (bKash, Nagad, COD).
3. **Timeline:** Its rapid installation, intuitive admin dashboard, and robust ecosystem enabled end-to-end store creation, product import, and payment testing within the 1 hour 30 minute assessment limit.

## Platform Comparison Matrix

| Feature / Criteria | WordPress + WooCommerce | OpenCart | Selected Choice |
